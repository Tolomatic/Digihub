How to Prepare Sierra Leonean Jollof Rice

Jollof rice is a popular West African dish prepared with rice, tomatoes, onions, peppers, and spices. In Sierra Leone, it is commonly served at home and during special occasions.

Ingredients

The following ingredients are needed to prepare approximately four servings of jollof rice.

  • 2 cups of long-grain rice
  • 3 medium-sized tomatoes
  • 2 tablespoons of tomato paste
  • 1 large onion
  • 1 red bell pepper
  • 1 small hot pepper (optional)
  • 3 tablespoons of vegetable oil
  • 2 cloves of garlic
  • 1 teaspoon of curry powder
  • 1 teaspoon of dried thyme
  • 2 to 3 cups of chicken or vegetable stock
  • Salt and seasoning to taste

Preparation Steps

Follow these steps in the correct order to prepare the rice.

  1. Wash the rice thoroughly and set it aside.
  2. Wash and chop the tomatoes, onion, bell pepper, and garlic.
  3. Blend the tomatoes and peppers into a smooth mixture.
  4. Heat the vegetable oil in a pot over medium heat.
  5. Add the chopped onion and garlic. Stir for about two minutes.
  6. Add the tomato paste and cook briefly, stirring to prevent burning.
  7. Pour in the blended tomato mixture and cook until it thickens.
  8. Add the curry powder, thyme, salt, and other seasonings.
  9. Add the washed rice and stir until it is coated with the sauce.
  10. Pour in enough stock to cook the rice. Cover the pot and reduce the heat.
  11. Allow the rice to cook gently for about 20 to 30 minutes, checking occasionally. Add a little hot water if necessary.
  12. Once the rice is tender and the liquid has been absorbed, turn off the heat. Allow it to rest briefly before serving.

Important Cooking and Safety Notes

Warning: Be careful when handling hot oil and boiling stock to avoid burns.

Food Safety: Wash your hands and cooking utensils before preparing the meal. Make sure all ingredients are properly cleaned.

Tip: Cook the rice over low heat after adding the stock to prevent burning.

Tip: The amount of stock may vary depending on the type of rice used.

Common Cooking Terms

The following terms are commonly used when preparing jollof rice.

Simmer
To cook food gently in liquid at a temperature just below boiling.
Blend
To mix ingredients until they form a smooth mixture.
Season
To add salt, herbs, or spices to improve the taste of food.
Stir
To move ingredients around in a pot so they mix and cook evenly.

Serving Suggestion

Jollof rice can be served with fried chicken, grilled fish, or a fresh vegetable salad. It is best enjoyed while warm.
